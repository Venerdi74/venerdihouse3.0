Where to eat in Alghero: restaurants recommended by Venerdì House

Restaurants, trattorias and practical places selected by Venerdì House to help you plan dinners, quick lunches and special evenings during your stay.

If you are looking for a restaurant in Alghero, this guide brings together a few useful places to consider during your stay. It is not an absolute ranking and it is not a sponsored list: it is a practical selection for guests staying at Venerdì House, designed to help you choose between seafood, local dishes and more relaxed options.

Alghero’s food scene is closely linked to the sea, Sardinian tradition and the city’s Catalan soul. That is why it can be worth mixing an evening in the old town, a restaurant on or near the seafront, and a simpler stop for lunch after the beach.

When choosing where to eat, it is better to look beyond the menu displayed outside and beyond generic rankings. What matters is understanding what kind of food the restaurant really serves, what atmosphere it offers and whether it suits the evening you have in mind. A beautiful setting or a sea view is not enough if the food does not live up to it.

Opening days, opening hours, price ranges and seasonal schedules can change: before going, especially in high season, it is always worth checking and booking ahead.

Restaurants recommended by Venerdì House

The order of the restaurants is not a ranking. The places below are presented as practical ideas to consider depending on the area, the type of food and the kind of evening you want to organise.

  • Ristorante Enhorabona

    Via Giuseppe Garibaldi, 101 - Seafood and Italian cuisine - Mid-range

    A convenient choice if you want to stay in the Lido area without moving too far towards the old town. It works well for a relaxed dinner after a day at the beach or for an easy evening close to the sea.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Ristorante Mabrouk

    Via Santa Barbara, 4 - Seafood and tradition - High-end

    One of the better-known names in town for a traditional seafood dinner. Before planning your evening, it is worth checking the current formula, availability and booking options.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Trattoria Lo Romani - Da Vittoria e Gigi

    Via Lazzaretto, 9 - Sardinian food and local dishes - Mid-range

    Here the focus is on local cooking in a simple, characteristic setting. It can be a good choice when you want to alternate seafood with more traditional Sardinian flavours.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Sa Mandra

    SP 44, 1 - Agriturismo and traditional Sardinian dishes - Mid-range

    Compared with restaurants in town, Sa Mandra offers an experience more closely tied to Sardinian country cooking and inland flavours. It requires a trip outside Alghero, so it is better to plan ahead.

    Find on Google Maps

  • La Piccola Cucina

    Via Sant'Agostino, 44 - Italian cuisine and carefully prepared dishes - Mid-range

    A good option if you are looking for well-prepared dishes in a central area, with an atmosphere suited to a quiet dinner. It can work well when you want something pleasant without moving too far away from the usual routes through the centre.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Lo Chalet Ristorante Wine & Bar

    Viale I Maggio, 15 - Restaurant and wine bar - Mid-range

    A useful choice for an evening near the beach area, with a livelier formula than a classic restaurant. It suits those looking for a relaxed place, a wine bar atmosphere, cocktails and a freer pace.

    Find on Google Maps

  • La Saletta

    Via Fratelli Kennedy, 27/A - Refined seafood cooking - High-end

    A smaller and more intimate setting, suited to a quiet dinner and a slower rhythm. It may appeal to couples or to anyone looking for a more considered evening.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Ristorante Da Musciora Danilo Delrio

    Via Giuseppe Mazzini, 77 - Fine dining and special occasions - High-end

    If you want a more elegant dinner or a special evening, this is one of the names to consider more carefully. It is not a place to choose casually at the last minute: booking and planning ahead is recommended.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Il Refettorio

    Vicolo Adami, 47 - Italian and Mediterranean cuisine - Mid-range

    In the old town, Il Refettorio offers a carefully prepared menu and a location that works well for an evening among Alghero’s narrow streets. It pairs nicely with a walk through the heart of the city before or after dinner.

    Find on Google Maps

  • Il Pavone

    Piazza Sulis, 3 - Seafood and Alghero-style cooking - High-end

    A historic and recognisable restaurant, closely linked to seafood and local tradition. It is more suited to a classic dinner in the centre of Alghero than to a quick, casual meal.

    Find on Google Maps

FAQ - Where to eat in Alghero

Where can you eat local food in Alghero?
For flavours closer to local tradition, Trattoria Lo Romani and Sa Mandra are worth considering. For a more classic seafood dinner, Mabrouk and Il Pavone are useful names to keep in mind.

When should you book restaurants in Alghero?
It is best to book in high season, at weekends and whenever you have a specific restaurant in mind. For the most requested places, booking a few days ahead is usually safer.

Is it always better to eat in the old town?
Not always. The old town is atmospheric for dinner, but the seafront and Lido area can be more convenient if you want to stay close to Venerdì House or avoid longer transfers.

Which restaurants are more suitable for families?
For an easier evening, Enhorabona or Lo Chalet may be worth considering. If you are travelling with children, it is always best to check menus, opening times and availability before booking.

Where can you eat seafood in Alghero?
Mabrouk, Il Pavone, Enhorabona, La Saletta and Da Musciora are among the restaurants to consider if you are looking for seafood or a menu with a stronger focus on the sea.

Are there restaurants close to Venerdì House?
Yes. In the Lido and seafront area there are several places that are easy to reach. The old town is also accessible with a longer walk, by bus or with a short transfer.

Where can you eat something quick in Alghero?
For a simple lunch or an easy break, you can look for focaccerie, sandwich places and casual spots near the harbour or the centre.
