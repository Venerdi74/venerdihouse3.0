Best beaches in Alghero

A practical guide to plan your beach holiday between beaches close to the centre, more scenic bays and quieter spots surrounded by nature.

Alghero and its beaches

If you are looking for the best beaches in Alghero and want to understand which ones to choose during your holiday, this guide can help you decide according to distance, comfort, atmosphere and the kind of beach day you want.

One of the reasons Alghero is so popular is that its coastline offers very different options within a relatively short distance. Some beaches are perfect for visitors who want to stay close to town, while others are better for those looking for wider views, crystal-clear water and a more natural setting. Knowing the difference helps you plan each day more easily and choose the beach that suits you best.

  • Beaches near the centre: Lido, Maria Pia
  • More scenic beaches: Le Bombarde, Lazzaretto
  • More natural settings: Mugoni, Porto Ferro

For anyone staying in town, this is one of the great advantages of Alghero: you can enjoy the sea without giving up the comfort of the city. If you are looking for a holiday apartment in Alghero near the sea, understanding the character of each beach makes it much easier to organise a more relaxed and enjoyable holiday.

Lido di Alghero

Lido di Alghero is one of the easiest beaches to choose if you are staying in town. It is convenient, easy to reach and ideal for families, couples and anyone who wants to combine a few hours on the beach with a walk along the seafront. The shallow water also makes it a very comfortable option for visitors travelling with children.

Maria Pia

Not far away, Maria Pia is one of the most loved beaches in the area thanks to its fine, pale sand, shallow clear water and beautiful pine forest behind it. The dunes and Mediterranean vegetation make it especially pleasant for those who want to spend a long and comfortable day by the sea without going too far from Alghero.

Le Bombarde

Le Bombarde is probably the most famous beach on the Alghero coast. Here you find golden sand, very clear water and a setting that works well both for families and for travellers who enjoy snorkelling. Beach clubs, bars and restaurants make it a great choice for anyone looking for a classic Sardinian beach experience with beautiful scenery and a lively atmosphere.

Lazzaretto

Close to Le Bombarde, Lazzaretto offers a slightly different feeling. The landscape is more varied, with smaller coves, turquoise water and a more intimate atmosphere. Many visitors choose it for its crystal-clear sea, panoramic views and the balance between useful services, open space and a slightly more sheltered feel than broader and busier beaches.

Mugoni

Mugoni has a calmer and more relaxed character. Sheltered from the wind, with soft sand, usually calm water and a pine forest that reaches almost to the beach, it is ideal for those who want a quieter beach day. It is especially appreciated by visitors who enjoy shade, a slower rhythm and a more peaceful seaside setting.

Porto Ferro

Porto Ferro feels wilder and more open. Red sandstone cliffs, Aragonese towers and a less urban setting give it a very distinctive character. It is often preferred by those who want to discover a different side of Alghero, and it is especially attractive for visitors who enjoy broad views, coastal walks, surfing and a more untamed atmosphere.

Which beach should you choose in Alghero?

If you want comfort and easy access from town, Lido and Maria Pia are among the best options, especially for families and for visitors who want to move around easily. If you are looking for spectacular sea colours and more iconic views, Le Bombarde and Lazzaretto are among the most famous choices. For a quieter and more natural setting, Mugoni and Porto Ferro are two of the best alternatives.

Choosing the right beach also depends on the kind of holiday you want: easy days close to the centre, more scenic outings with beautiful views, or quieter corners to enjoy at a slower pace. That is why staying in a practical part of Alghero can make the whole experience much easier.

Other beaches near Alghero

If you want to explore beyond the main Alghero coastline, there are other beaches worth considering during your holiday. Spiaggia della Speranza, south of Alghero, is appreciated for its quieter atmosphere and open coastal views. Argentiera has a very distinctive charm thanks to its former mining landscape and its wilder stretch of coast.

You can also reach Le Saline, ideal for those who enjoy wide open spaces and fewer crowds, and La Pelosa in Stintino, one of the most famous beaches in Sardinia, known for its pale sand and Caribbean-like sea colours. These are good options for a day trip if you want to combine the beaches of Alghero with other spectacular parts of the north-western coast.
