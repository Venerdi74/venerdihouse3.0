Our apartment in Alghero

Venerdì House is an 80 sqm apartment in Alghero, located on the second floor with a lift, about 100 metres from San Giovanni Beach, easy to reach on foot, and close to the historic centre. It is designed for couples, families and small groups looking for a bright, practical and relaxing apartment in a convenient area to enjoy at any time of day.

The apartment includes two bedrooms, a living room, an equipped kitchen, a bathroom and balconies, with functional spaces that make it easy to alternate beach days, quiet evenings in and nights out in town with more comfort and freedom. Air conditioning and free Wi-Fi make the stay even easier and more enjoyable.

The location near the beach, supermarkets, bars and restaurants makes Venerdì House a convenient way to enjoy Alghero with independence, without giving up peace and comfort. Direct booking also lets you contact us directly and receive availability and prices without intermediaries.

Our rooms

The rooms at Venerdì House are designed to make your stay feel simple, comfortable and calm after a day in Alghero.

The main double bedroom is spacious and bright, ideal for anyone looking for a welcoming and relaxing space to return to after the beach, an evening walk or dinner in the historic centre.

The second bedroom has twin beds that can be joined into a double, a flexible solution that makes the apartment suitable for couples, families and small groups visiting Alghero.
